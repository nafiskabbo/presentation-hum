# Motion brief

For: `output/Drone_Food_Delivery_Bangladesh_Group_Presentation-v6.pptx`

## What is honest about motion here

Native PowerPoint animation timelines cannot be written reliably with the current toolchain.
We do not claim otherwise, and we do not pretend a fade was added that was not.

| Method | What it is | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Matched static builds | Identical layout across consecutive slides so the eye reads one object changing | Slides 16 to 18, 19 to 22, 29 to 33, 34 to 41 |
| 2. Motion video inserts | Short silent MP4 with a poster frame, generated from real photographs | Slide 1 (title), slide 2 (corridor) |
| 3. Frame based builds | A sequence revealed over several static frames | Slides 2, 4 |
| 4. OOXML transitions | Slide level fades only, applied by post processing and validated | Not used. We have not validated it, so we did not ship it. |

Method 4 is listed so the claim is not overstated. The deck is designed to read fully with no
motion at all: every argument is carried by a diagram, a table or a native chart that reads
without a trigger.

## No third party drone video

The only freely licensed drone footage available is wilderness and farmland training. One clip
carries a United States Bureau of Land Management logo on its end card. Both are off message for
a Dhaka food business and the branding rule forbids it. So no third party drone video is used.

Motion is instead built from the real delivery-drone photographs already in the deck, and from
one clearly labelled schematic.

## The three assets

Generated by `build/make_motion.py`, which is committed. All silent, 1600x900, H.264, poster
frame supplied, and the poster is also drawn as a real image underneath the video so the slide
still reads if playback is blocked.

| Asset | Source | What it is | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hero_push.mp4` | `hero_package_drop.jpg`, a real Zipline delivery photograph | Slow 8 second push in on a real delivery drone releasing a package | 707 KB |
| `corridor.mp4` | drawn frame by frame, not a photograph | Schematic corridor loop with a drone travelling hub to pad. Captioned on the slide as an illustration, never as flight footage | 59 KB |
| `dhaka_fly.mp4` | `dhaka_aerial.jpg`, a real Dhaka aerial photograph | Slow 9 second drift over the Kuril Flyover junction | 674 KB |

`dhaka_fly.mp4` is built and available but is not currently placed on a slide. If Section 1 or 5
is expanded, it is the natural fit for the market or environment slide.

## Poster frame fallback

Every video insert is drawn as two objects: the poster JPEG as a normal image, and the video on
top with the same rectangle. In PowerPoint the video plays. If the codec is missing or autoplay
is blocked, the photograph underneath is what the audience sees. This was verified in the PDF
export, where the drone is visible behind the player control.

## Placeholder register

There are no placeholder frames in this deck. Every image is a real, sourced, credited
photograph, and every video is built from one of those photographs or from a labelled schematic.

## Rejected assets, recorded so they are not retried

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and then deleted because the subject was wrong:

| File | Why it was rejected |
|---|---|
| A Hasselblad camera photograph returned for a quadcopter search | It is a camera, not a drone |
| A fixed wing aircraft on a dirt runway | Wrong vehicle class, and reads as a bush plane |
| A hand throwing a toy quadcopter in a field | Amateur, and undermines a business case |
| A park with a person cycling in the distance | Not a delivery rider |
| BLM drone training footage, two clips | United States government branding, wilderness subject |
| Phosphate mining, ISS interiors, a 1935 Tokyo delivery man | Search returned nonsense for the query |

## If more motion is wanted

`build/make_motion.py` takes any still and produces a push or drift. Adding an asset is: drop the
photograph in `media/img/`, add a `ken_burns` call, add a `CREDIT` entry in `build/design.js`,
and list it on the Image credits slide. Do not add motion to any chart. A native chart with an
animation is a native chart with a bug risk.
