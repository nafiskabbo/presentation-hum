## Photographs deliberately removed from v7

These were in earlier drafts and are gone, because they were either off message or weak:

- `drone_quad.jpg`, a consumer DJI product shot. Marketing imagery, not evidence.
- `courier_van.jpg`, which carried platform branding. We do not want a competitor's logo in our deck.
- `dhaka_building.jpg`, a generic residential block. It said nothing.
- `rajshahi_campus.jpg`, which turned out to be a railway approach and gate, not a campus.
- `rajshahi_college.jpg`, an institutional building shot at a distance where the people on the
  steps are identifiable. Requirement.md section 11 forbids photographing identifiable people
  without consent, so it was dropped on privacy grounds rather than quality grounds.

If a replacement campus or university image is ever wanted, the constraint is that no
identifiable individual may appear. Institutional building exteriors at a distance are fine.

## Motion

Two silent MP4s, generated by `build/make_motion.py` from the deck's own photographs:

- `media/motion/dhaka_fly.mp4`, a slow push over the Dhaka aerial photograph, on slide 9.
- `media/motion/corridor.mp4`, a slow drift across the Dhaka rooftop photograph, on slide 15.
- `media/motion/hero_push.mp4`, generated for the title slide but not embedded in v7, because the
  title now uses a static photograph with a clean white panel.

Each MP4 has a poster JPEG drawn underneath it as a real image in the slide, so the slide still
looks correct if the machine has no video codec or playback is blocked.

There is no third party drone video in the deck. None exists under a free licence that is both on
message and free of government or competitor branding, and the brief does not allow a licensed
clip to be used without permission.

## Fallback image prompts

`media/asset_prompt_sheet.md` holds image generation prompts. They are a fallback only, for the
client's own future use. Nothing in the shipped deck is AI generated, and requirement.md
section 11 requires real licensed photographs wherever one exists.

## Why no credits slide

Requirement.md section 7 says not to build a references slide or an image credits slide, because
the client supplies those separately. Short credits are still printed on the slides themselves,
so the deck is properly attributed even on its own. `media/img/credits.json` stays in step with
the files on disk and is the source the credit lines are written from.
